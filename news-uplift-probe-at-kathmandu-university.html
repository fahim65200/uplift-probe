Uplift Probe at Kathmandu University

Uplift Probe held a series of meetings at Kathmandu University aimed at building collaboration between Bangladesh and Nepal.

We began with the Forum for Environment Conservation and Management (FECAM), where both organizations shared their upcoming agendas and explored joint initiatives.

We then met Dr. Kumud Raj Kafle, Chairman and Head of the Department of Environmental Science and Engineering. His welcome and his commitment to academic collaboration gave us a strong base for future research and educational partnerships.

Finally, we met the team behind Project Sanrakshan, an initiative for environmental preservation, and agreed to keep working together.

We look forward to organizing collaborative research programmes, conferences and seminars.
