About Uplift Probe

A research organization that gives students a place to learn research by doing it.

Why we exist

Undergraduates in Bangladesh get few chances to take part in real research. Most meet it for the first time in a final-year thesis, with little guidance and less practice.

Uplift Probe was founded to change that. We recruit research associates, teach them the methods, and put them to work on papers alongside people who have published before. We also bring in professors and working researchers to teach.

Alongside the training, we share what research says with a wider audience through briefs, webinars and school workshops.

Our mission

  • Ask the hard questions

    Challenge the boundaries of knowledge and take on questions that are tough or left unanswered.

  • Publish what we find

    Publish research papers in respected journals and share knowledge with the global scientific community.

  • Widen the conversation

    Publish articles that carry our research further and encourage intellectual dialogue.

  • Build researchers

    Give students the training, mentoring and platform they need to become researchers themselves.

Meet the people behind it

A core team of five, a senior researcher and a cohort of research associates from Bangladesh and abroad.
