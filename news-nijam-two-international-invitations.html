Nijam invited to two international summits

Our Senior Researcher, Md. Nijam Uddin Sarkar, was invited to two international events in 2025.

Future Action Summit 2025, Bangkok: a fully funded invitation to join leaders and innovators working on sustainable development.

4th Japan Youth Summit 2025, Tokyo: the Gold Package Scholarship to attend a summit on child development inequalities and sustainable innovation.

Both invitations reflect his work in environmental research and youth empowerment.
