Partners

Organizations we work with in Bangladesh and Nepal.

FECAM, Kathmandu University

The Forum for Environment Conservation and Management (FECAM) is based at Kathmandu University, Nepal. Uplift Probe and FECAM signed a Memorandum of Understanding in 2025 for a six-month collaboration on environmental issues.

Our first joint event was the World Environment Day webinar “Unplastic the Planet: A Joint Call from Nepal and Bangladesh,” with speakers and hosts from both countries.

Department of Environmental Science and Engineering, Kathmandu University

We met the Chairman and Head of Department, Dr. Kumud Raj Kafle, to discuss research and educational collaboration between Bangladesh and Nepal.

Project Sanrakshan
