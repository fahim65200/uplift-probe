Fahim invited to present at iCWEE-2025 in Sydney

Mohammed Fahim Murshed, Founder and Executive Director of Uplift Probe, was invited to present his research at the 4th International Conference on Water and Environmental Engineering (iCWEE-2025) at Western Sydney University, Australia.

The conference waived his full registration fee in recognition of the significance and innovation of the work.
