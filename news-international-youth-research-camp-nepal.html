Fahim joins the International Youth Research Camp in Nepal

Our Founder and Executive Director, Mohammed Fahim Murshed, attended the International Youth Research Camp in Nepal on a partial scholarship.

The programme included visits to Kathmandu University, Pokhara University and the Embassy of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh in Kathmandu.

The experience fed directly into Uplift Probe’s work, including our partnership with FECAM.
