Best Paper Award (student category) at iCWEE-2025

Our Founder and Executive Director, Mohammed Fahim Murshed, won the Best Paper Award (student category) at the 4th International Conference on Water and Environmental Engineering (iCWEE-2025), held at Western Sydney University, Australia.

The paper is titled “Assessing the Potentiality of Rainwater Harvesting System as a Source of Drinking Water in the Coastal Regions of Bangladesh.”

The award recognizes work on sustainable water solutions and shows that research from Bangladesh has a place in the global conversation.
